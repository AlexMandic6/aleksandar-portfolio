## Problem

Booking a personal service often means comparing the service, its duration and a suitable time. This project explores how to make those choices clear within one flow.

## Product direction

The intended experience lets an owner define services, treatment durations and availability. A visitor could then choose a service and a time while keeping appointment details in view. These are design goals, not claims about shipped functionality.
